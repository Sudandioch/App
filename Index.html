How Survey Tasks Look

Each survey task is simple, clear, and rewarding:

  • 📝 Question Type: Multiple-choice, rating, or short answer
  • ⏳ Time: 1-2 minutes
  • 🎯 Points: Vary depending on survey type
  • 💰 Conversion: 10 survey points = 10 Kshs
  • 💡 Tips: Read carefully to maximize rewards
  • 🔥 Completion: Immediate points credited
  • 🏆 Maximum Activations per Package:
    • Standard Surveys – 3 activations
    • Premium Surveys – 5 activations
